# Unemployer artwork

Built-in imagegen generated the original logo and matching artwork. The final banner is a 2560x1440 PNG export (under 6 MB); the wordmark and tagline fit the centered mobile-safe region. The original generated sources are retained. The profile logo is square and designed for a circular crop.

Files:
- unemployer-logo.png: channel avatar
- unemployer-banner.png: upload-ready channel banner
- channel-description.txt: 723-character description ready to paste into YouTube Studio

The assets are created locally; they have not been installed in YouTube Studio. Current OAuth access covers video uploads and channel read access, not channel customization.

Final prompt set, built-in imagegen:
1. Logo: a bold geometric mint U whose right stroke rises into an upward arrow, centered on dark navy, generous circular-crop margins, no text, flat original mark readable at avatar size.
2. Banner: preserve the U-arrow identity, navy/mint/cream palette; standard 16:9 canvas; central compact lockup with exact text UNEMPLOYER, Your next opportunity starts here., and INTERNSHIPS / FRESHER JOBS / CAREER UPDATES; all important content within the centered safe area; decorative paths outside; no extra text.
3. Final refinement: expand vertically to widescreen, scale the lockup to the mobile-safe center, preserve identity and spelling; request a high-resolution export. The generator returned a smaller raster, so the final PNG was exported at YouTube's required dimensions without changing the design.
